## Cross-corridor comparison (south-Liverpool rail anchors, 2024/25)

| Station                 | Postcode | Entries+exits 2024/25 | Trajectory |
|-------------------------|----------|-----------------------|------------|
| Mossley Hill            | L18 8AG  | 199,000               | +9% YoY    |
| Aigburth                | L17 7AA  | 597,000               | -6% YoY    |
| St Michaels             | L17 7BD  | 1,015,000             | -9% YoY    |
| Cressington             | L19 0AS  | 531,000               | -3% YoY    |
| Liverpool South Parkway | L19/L24  | 2,164,000             | +7% YoY    |

LSP is the **second-busiest non-city-centre Merseyrail station after Liverpool
Central / Lime Street / Moorfields / James Street**, larger than the entire
combined Aigburth-corridor anchor (1.81m / yr). Its growth is the only positive
trajectory in 2024/25 across the south-Liverpool set, driven by airport bus
interchange (80A / 86A to Liverpool John Lennon) plus Avanti / TPE / EMR / TfW
long-distance services on the City Line and West Coast Main Line.

Cressington is the surprise of the L19 pull — at 531k it is **2.7× Mossley Hill**
and within 12% of Aigburth's 597k, despite serving the smaller Cressington Park
Conservation Area residential pocket. Service is 4 trains per hour each way to
Hunts Cross / Ormskirk on the Merseyrail Northern Line; the station is staffed
with a booking office, waiting room and live departure screens — closer to a
neighbourhood-rail format than an unstaffed halt. The 2024/25 dip is small
(-2.6%) and the trajectory remains 191% above 2020/21 baseline.

## What this means for the L19 candidates

**QS-20260508-013 — Aigburth Rd L19, 1,466 sqft Class E @ £18.5k pa via SK
Real Estate Liverpool Tom Pearson (Property 4607).** Algorithmic 56 [Reject]
and the closest L19 candidate to the Watch threshold; manual-override candidate
at line 308. The Aigburth-Rd-A561-corridor segment in lower-numbers L19 0xx
sits in the **walk-up shadow of three rail anchors**:

- St Michaels (L17 7BD) at the Aigburth-fringe — 1.015m / yr (closest to upper
  L19 0xx numbers ~600m walk).
- Aigburth (L17 7AA) one stop further north — 597k / yr.
- **Cressington (L19 0AS) at the L19 catchment heart** — 531k / yr.

Combined three-station rail-anchored catchment around the lower-Aigburth-Rd
A561 corridor between St Michaels and Cressington is **2.14m passenger
movements / yr**, comparable to Liverpool South Parkway alone but distributed
across a 1.5-mile Aigburth/Cressington walk-up envelope rather than a single
parkway-interchange node. This is a genuine L17/L18-comparable rail-anchored
context — the 4-7% Muslim density borough cap is the right ceiling on the
Brand Fit pillar but the Catchment / Footfall pillars are arguably under-stated
in the algorithmic 56 score. Read: re-engage manual-override consideration at
QS-013 with **Cressington-walk-up overlay** material; expected Watch-band
60-68 ceiling on confirmation of (a) exact unit postcode walk-time to
Cressington station, (b) below-market £12.6 psf Cat-B-payback envelope, (c)
SK Real Estate pack diligence.

**QS-20260508-014 — Unit 1 Matchworks Speke Rd L19 2RF, 8,803 sqft via Urban
Splash Manchester.** Algorithmic 31 [Reject] driven primarily by 8,803 sqft =
4× DOCTRINE §3 Flagship-band ceiling and business-park-canteen format. Rail
data does **not** rescue this candidate: although Matchworks sits directly
across Speke Rd from Liverpool South Parkway (2.16m passenger movements / yr),
LPY is a **transit-and-airport-bus interchange, not a destination retail
station**. ~10% of LPY's 2024/25 throughput (232k) is pure interchange — never
sets foot outside the gates. The remaining 1.93m entry+exit traffic is
predominantly Northern Line commuters bound for Liverpool Central or City Line
+ WCML passengers boarding for Manchester / Crewe / London or stepping off
the airport bus — none of these dwell-times suit a hospitality-led Yemeni
flagship, and Matchworks' creative-industries tenant base wants a canteen
format Qamaria is not. Hold the algorithmic Reject.

**QS-20260508-015 — 140 Speke Rd L19 2PH via Urban Splash Manchester /
PropList.** Algorithmic 46 [Reject]. Same LPY-fringe context as QS-014 but in
secondary Satellite-band sizing — adjusted for format the rail anchor is
modestly more relevant, but the Speke-Rd-A561 industrial / business-park
context still mismatches Qamaria's hospitality-led brand essence and the
Brand Fit pillar holds.

**QS-20260508-016 — Unit 3/4 Gateway Complex 17 St Mary's Rd L19 2NJ via M
Core Property / Completely Retail 63834.** Algorithmic 38 [Reject]. Garston
parade is anchored by Liverpool South Parkway (~10 min walk SW) and St
Michaels (~15 min walk N) but the parade itself is the secondary-mass
discount-retail Aldi / Iceland / Poundland co-tenant context that caps Brand
Fit irrespective of footfall. Rail data does not shift this score.

**Generic L19 read.** Cressington's 531k 2024/25 figure is the unexpected lift
in the data: the L19 catchment is **not** rail-poor. Combined L19/L24
rail-anchor footfall exposed to the Aigburth-fringe / Garston / Speke-fringe
sub-pitches is 2.69m passenger movements / yr (Cressington 531k + LPY 2.16m).
But the distribution is bifurcated: Cressington serves an affluent residential
micro-pocket aligned with a hospitality-led format, while LPY serves a
parkway-interchange demographic that does not absorb destination-Yemeni
hospitality. The L19 catchment-quality story is therefore **Aigburth-fringe
via Cressington > Garston via St Michaels >> Speke-fringe via LPY**, and the
QSFI ordering on the four candidates (QS-013 56 > QS-015 46 > QS-016 38 >
QS-014 31) is corroborated by the rail data.
