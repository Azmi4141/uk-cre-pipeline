## What this means for the L18 / L17 candidates

**QS-20260508-012 — Rose Lane L18 8AG, 516 sqft Transit @ £17k pa via Smith & Sons
+ LSH twin-listing.** The station-anchor thesis underpinning this candidate's
Transit-band score holds: Mossley Hill is the smallest of the three Aigburth-corridor
stations (4-7× smaller than Aigburth and St Michaels) but it is on a **steady
multi-year growth trajectory** — every year since the pandemic baseline. 2024/25
already 199k vs. 2023/24 183k = +9% YoY, consistent with the L18 catchment-recovery
read on Allerton parade chain-coffee absorption. 501 average daily station boardings
+ alightings is light for a Transit-band Qamaria pitch (DOCTRINE §3 Transit format
expects "high turnover" — Aigburth's 1,748/day or St Michaels' 3,058/day are closer
to that envelope). Read: Rose Lane is genuinely transit-anchored but the anchor is
**neighbourhood-rail not destination-rail**; the 516 sqft envelope and £33 psf rent
are sized correctly for that, not over-sized. No re-band warranted on the QSFI 58
[Reject] sit; algorithmic Transit-band Brand Fit ceiling holds.

**QS-20260508-018 — 82-84 Aigburth Rd L17 7BN, 1,440 sqft Satellite @ £20k pa via
Onyx Real Estate / LoopNet 33849938.** Aigburth Rd corridor sits between Aigburth
station (638k passengers 2023/24) and St Michaels (1.1m). Combined corridor anchor
is **1.75 million passengers / yr** within an L17 walk-up of one or both stations
— the highest rail-anchored catchment surfaced across the L# arc to date excluding
L1/L2/L3 city-centre stations. 82-84 Aigburth Rd is closer to St Michaels (Cressington
Road junction is ~600m walk to St Michaels) than to Aigburth station. That density
materially supports the QS-018 Watch-band rating (61) — the Cressington-fringe
pitch trades off some of the L17 7 ward halal density vs. L8/L15 against the
strongest L17/L18 rail-anchor catchment, and the rail data confirms the trade is
real not narrative.

**Generic L17/L18 read:** the three stations together delivered 1.94 million passenger
movements in 2023/24 and 1.81 million in 2024/25 — a "soft year" but still ~50%
above 2021/22 baseline. Recovery is not over (post-pandemic curve still climbing
in 4 of 6 station-years observed). Footfall absorption capacity for a hospitality-
led Yemeni format on Aigburth Road A561 between St Michaels and Aigburth stations
is materially under-stated by Liverpool LA borough-level proxies which average across
hollowed-out city-fringe wards.
